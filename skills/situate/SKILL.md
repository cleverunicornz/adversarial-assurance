# Situate - The During Register

## Authority And Boundary

`architecture/development-lifecycle/AGENTS.md` owns the register boundary;
`architecture/development-lifecycle/situating.md` owns every Situate semantic,
coordinate, trigger, refusal, and non-authority rule. Read both against current
source. This skill is a compact conduct disclosure, not a second specification.

Situate is development-lifecycle-local. It is not a graph act, work-protocol
verb, lane transition, instruction channel, or permission to proceed. This
skill creates no situator, operation, command, service, policy, comparator, or
state carrier. Where no commissioned Situate seam exists, do not invent one.

## Compose Or Assess

Before accepting a composition, resolve from the existing commissioned seam:

- one scope inside Commission through Seal, one attributed author, and one
  target actor;
- the first satisfied admitted trigger and its canonical scope-local evidence;
- the exact immutable context-policy generation already bound by the
  commission; and
- the next lineage-local sequence and canonical source references.

The caller supplies observations, never the trigger class or policy values.
Every durable fact remains owned by its canonical source.

The sensorium law is invariant: a situation reports only true facts. Synthetic
urgency, manufactured scarcity, or any fabricated state presented as fact is
malfunction of the situating actor. Content is attributed and source-linked;
it never instructs, steers, grants authority, reports correctness, impersonates
a notice, or makes a declared next edge binding.

An admitted situation is immutable. Refresh or correction appends the exact
successor required by the owning architecture; it never edits history. Recall
is admitted only under the bound policy generation and its measured frontier
and cap, never under caller-selected constants.

## Refuse Without Authority

If any coordinate, source, policy, trigger, content, recall, or sequence check
fails, return the first applicable typed refusal from `situating.md`. Produce
no event or artifact, mutate no prior truth, and do not reinterpret the named
door as permission. Situate supplies awareness only; the owning lifecycle
alone controls obligations, verdicts, gates, and workflow state.
