# Collapser

A collapser application is a bound relationship, not a tool name:

```text
promise
  -> live possibility at a named boundary
  -> exact qualified instrument
  -> oracle judgment
  -> retained witness
  -> gate placement plus named residual
```

`$collapse-graph` names commissioned classes and relationships. This
skill binds one graph edge to one real surface. Writing `buf`, `DST`,
`nextest`, or `playwright` without that binding is an ornamental route:
it states a method family while leaving the possibility uncollapsed.

Load `$search` and resolve every claimed identity, boundary, control,
gate, and witness from current source plus the owning risk entry. A graph
node proposes a relationship; the current tree disposes the application.

## The Application Questions

Answer all of these in the stage-owned artifact. They are semantic
obligations, not a new file format or validator grammar.

1. **Possibility.** What exact alternative behavior is being forced down?
2. **Instrument identity.** What binary, version or hash, configuration,
   flags, category, exclusions, baseline, topology, seed policy, and
   comparator make this the instrument actually used?
3. **Applicability and subject.** Does the instrument receive a supplied
   subject or construct one, what may it transform, and why can that exact
   path reach the named boundary and decisive behavior?
4. **Fidelity.** Where does its model stop, and what claim may not be
   credited beyond that edge?
5. **Qualification.** Did this exact identity pass known-answer controls
   in both polarities, with each control reaching the decisive predicate
   under `$oracle`? Where are those witnesses?
6. **Oracle.** What comparator judges the manufactured event against the
   promise?
7. **Witness.** What producer-bound actual and declared transformation path
   survive success, refusal, partial failure, and replay under `$witness`?
8. **Gate and residual.** Where does the application run again, and what
   possibility remains outside its construction, reach, rejection, or
   detection?

A route is honest only when a reader can answer those questions from
current source and pinned evidence without reconstructing intent.
Give each commissioned application a stable artifact-local reference so
tasks, proofs, validation, and later review can point to the same binding.
The reference syntax is prose-owned; do not add a validator grammar merely
to number the relationship.

## Subject Origin

Known-answer controls and delivery applications answer different questions.
Controls disclose whether the comparator discriminates. Subject origin
discloses whether the containing application can reach the commissioned
boundary without authoring the behavior it later credits.

### Binding Invariants

Subject origin and every permitted subject transformation are part of the
qualified application identity:

- **Supplied subject.** The behavior under judgment originates outside the
  apparatus. The apparatus may invoke, observe, copy, or perform declared
  representation transformations, but it does not add, repair, or substitute
  the behavior it later credits. Retain its identity at ingress and after
  every subject transformation; `$witness` separately binds the producer's
  resulting actual and producer-to-oracle transformations.
- **Constructed subject.** The apparatus authors material behavior it later
  judges. Its controls qualify that construction and comparator within their
  fidelity envelope; they do not establish that the same apparatus can judge a
  future supplied subject without authoring the promised behavior.

An application may claim only the subject origin and transformations its
controls qualified. A required relationship outside that envelope is an
unqualified application and cannot satisfy a 3000 lock; genesis or
requalification belongs to 2000.

### Decision Cues

Synthetic, copied, patched, or quarantined subjects can qualify comparator
discrimination when both polarities reach the decisive predicate. Their
location or construction does not, by itself, decide application suitability.
Likewise, absence of the future product is not a lock defect: a pre-product
application may qualify a supplied-subject path with a non-product specimen.
Inspect who authored the behavior, which transformations occurred, and what
relationship the contract actually commissions. Record that reasoning whether
the cue produces a finding or is declined.

## Honest Dispositions

Resolve each live possibility through one of these meanings:

- **Collapsed by construction.** Cite the type, ownership rule,
  single-writer boundary, or seam that makes the alternative
  unrepresentable or unreachable. No runner is invented.
- **Held by a proven standing gate.** Name the exact current gate and
  qualified instrument identity. A configured command that is not
  actually blocking is not a standing gate.
- **Commissioned application.** Bind the qualified instrument to a
  contract task, oracle, expected witness path, and intended gate
  placement.
- **Declared residual.** Name why stronger collapse is not commissioned,
  the bounded domain left open, and the decision owner. Residual is
  honest incompleteness; an unexecuted tool name is not residual and not
  collapse.

Prefer the strongest applicable mechanism: unrepresentable, unreachable,
rejected, detected, then declared. Do not spend an expensive detector on
an alternative the design can remove.

## Capability-Class Closure

A remediation closes the demonstrated possibility, not merely its observed
spelling. A detector scoped to helper names, property assignments, or
particular syntax proves only those enumerated forms. Equivalent ways to
exercise the capability remain residual unless a type, ownership rule, seam,
or capability boundary makes them unreachable, or a qualified exhaustive
instrument rejects them. Match every closure claim to the actual domain of
the mechanism.

## Qualification And Change

An unproven instrument measures nothing. Treat all behavior-affecting
parts as one identity: executable, version or hash, configuration,
selection flags, exclusions, baseline, harness topology, schedule or seed
policy, comparator, subject mode, and permitted subject transformations. Any
material change makes the application unqualified until the exact changed
identity passes both controls:

- a known-bad case must produce the decisive failure; and
- a known-good case must produce the decisive pass.

Classify genesis and requalification of delivery or standing apparatus as
possible `2000` work; the gap creates no campaign. Selecting an unchanged,
already qualified instrument for a grounded Promise is ordinary commissioned
`3000` authoring. A human-invoked `7000`
campaign may qualify a review-local instrument through its independent
executor/validator pair, but that instrument remains review evidence; its
adoption into delivery or a standing gate is possible 2000 and 3000 work only
under separate human commission.
Qualification proves discrimination; it does not silently place a
repository-wide gate. Gate placement remains the contract's or human
decision owner's act.

No qualification result, gap, application finding, route, or gate decision
creates another branch, unit, review, proof subject, or stage.

Inside 3000 or 4000, the active Closure bounds which application relationship
is an assurance subject. Executing or validating a named application does not
commission proof of its compiler, runner, host, network, dependencies, or
other supporting means unless a positive-depth acceptance row names that
relationship.

## Stage Ownership

- **2000:** prove feasibility, originate or requalify apparatus, preserve
  both controls and the declared subject mode, and hand forward a candidate
  application. A spike does not create a promise or place a gate.
- **3000:** bind every live possibility in `Collapse Route` to an honest
  disposition. A commissioned application names its exact identity,
  subject mode, applicability, fidelity, qualification evidence, task,
  oracle, witness, gate placement, and residual before lock.
- **4000:** execute every commissioned application, regardless of language
  or surface class, against current source. Retain its exact identity,
  output, oracle verdict, witness, and residual. `4030` independently
  re-manufactures the same application; it does not substitute a nearby
  tool or broader claim.
- **7000:** begin only from explicit human invocation. Inventory and prefer
  proven applications from architecture, the locked contract, and the
  delivery record. A new or changed review instrument qualifies before
  its output can support a verdict. Review supplements delivery assurance;
  it never retroactively supplies a missing 3000 or 4000 obligation.

## Fidelity Examples

- A protobuf compatibility checker can collapse the configured schema and
  wire categories against its exact baseline. It cannot claim runtime
  behavior, and qualification does not imply merge-gate placement.
- A deterministic simulation harness can collapse schedules and faults
  within its modeled network, time, and storage seams. Its witness cannot
  establish provider behavior outside that envelope, and a bounded history
  checker judges only the invariant it names.

Resolve current pins, exclusions, gate posture, and fidelity from the tree
and risk register. These examples define boundaries, not current status.

## Relationships

- `$promise` defines what must hold.
- `$collapse-graph` commissions the mechanism classes and neighborhood.
- `$oracle` owns the comparator and its discrimination.
- `$witness` owns the retained observation.
- The contract owns selection and gate intent; execution owns current
  application evidence; the risk register owns volatile posture.

## This Is NOT

- Not a second collapse graph or an instrument catalog.
- Not an oracle: the application includes a judge but does not redefine it.
- Not a witness: output without a qualified comparator is still not data.
- Not a campaign router: assurance weight never auto-invokes `7000`.
- Not operational materialization: native development-machine convergence or
  a staging/production Nix closure and activation realizes a terminal artifact
  in 8000 but does not collapse that artifact's product behavior. Moon is a
  runner, not a collapser; its development proof deployment remains in the
  owning Build lane.
- Not a validator schema: agents exercise judgment over the eight questions.
