# Route

Record what the current unit cannot lawfully continue and name the possible
receiver and admission condition: onward, back to a missing predecessor, or
aside as a referral carrying evidence.

Route is classification, not commission. It does not create a branch, unit,
pass, delegate, review, repair, retry, or receiving act. The current unit stops
or continues only within its existing commission; the receiver begins only
under its own prior commission or an express human commission.

Refusal: unnamed receiver or condition; a self-supplied predecessor;
pursuing out-of-docket sightings; treating the route record as receiver
authority.

The possible receiver is named at the act, never carried as an automatic
standing edge.
