# Lane 3000 - Contracts

The lane where a grounded Promise becomes a commissioned obligation carrying
its owner, judge, and envelope. Before decomposition, the human agrees and the
author Posts one block-3000 Closure through `$underwrite`. That Closure counts
only 3020 and 3030 passes. The final pushed contract commit is the Lock.

Refusal: no landed Promise, unresolved required oracle, absent pushed 3000
Closure, or exhausted allowance. Record the exact classification and stop.
No refusal, route, or result creates 1000, 2000, 4000, 6000, another review, or
a successor.

During the bounded authoring loop, the 3000 writer may revise the contract and
Posts every candidate before another actor reviews it. After Lock, agents have
no contract or Closure edit authority except one exact human instruction on
the active 4000 lineage; that direct pushed edit creates no 3000 re-entry.

This lane authors no Nix file, command, output, package, profile, closure,
cache action, service realization, durable machine-placement decision,
staging/production deployment obligation, or Nix proof.

The Lock makes a separately commissioned 4000 Closure possible because the
task graph is then known. It does not commission that Closure or the 4000
lane.
