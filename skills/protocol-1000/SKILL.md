# Lane 1000 — Research

The lane for directed uncertainty answered by reading. A unit here mutates
nothing outside its own evidence and seals on a recorded verdict, including
the verdict that no action is needed. Settled human direction manufactures
no research predecessor.

Refusal: the moment answering requires a build, realization, mutation, or
empirical rig, the question leaves at the door for 2000. Nix source may be
read like any other source. A read-only Nix inspection or evaluation exists
here only when the human commissions that exact action and it runs outside the
development environment; it never builds, realizes, publishes, activates,
deploys, or mutates a profile.

Flow: entry of the promise arc — closed knowledge carries straight to 6000,
empirical uncertainty through 2000 first.
