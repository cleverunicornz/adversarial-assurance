# Promote

Convert non-binding material into a binding obligation at the human
gate. The human is the only actor of this verb; an agent prepares the
material and never performs the conversion.

The human may extend a budget, change a commission, park, discard, or make the
separate Promotion decision. None of those acts manufactures an accepting
validator disposition or rewrites one already emitted by its owner.

Refusal: any agent performing it; assembly from recollection.

Contrast: Commission — Promote is the ratification direction of that one
boundary. Conditionally meets: Gate, where the criterion is the human
decision.
