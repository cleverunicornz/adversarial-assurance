# Repair

Change what a contract means only inside authority already present: the active
bounded 3000 authoring commission, or one exact human instruction after Lock.
Post the changed candidate under `$git-policy` before anyone consumes it.

Repair is not a commission. It does not invoke validation, create a pass,
re-enter 3000, Cut a successor, or route work. Any review of the result requires
remaining active-Closure admission or an express human review request.

Refusal: no contract-meaning change, or no existing authority for the change.

Contrast: `$protocol-remediate`.

Index: `$git-policy` - The Work Protocol Vocabulary.
