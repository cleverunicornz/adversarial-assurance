# Reckon - The After Register

## Authority And Boundary

`architecture/development-lifecycle/AGENTS.md` owns the register boundary;
`architecture/development-lifecycle/reckoning.md` owns every Reckon semantic,
coordinate, axis, refusal, and non-authority rule. Read both against current
source. This skill is a compact conduct disclosure, not a second specification.

Reckon is one development-lifecycle-local, read-only post-Seal derivation. It
is not a graph act, work-protocol verb, lane transition, verdict, completion
record, or permission to begin later work. This skill creates no operation,
instrument, report service, or canonical state carrier. Where no commissioned
Reckon seam exists, do not invent one.

## Derive Or Assess

Before deriving a ledger, resolve one exact coordinate:

- one unit and its existing terminal Seal identity;
- the immutable sealed source commit and complete durable evidence identity
  set; and
- one immutable derivation-basis identity owning the hindsight set, cost
  units, axis rules, classification rules, and residual.

Agent memory, an editable pull request, a current workspace, an unpinned
branch, and uncited later knowledge are not inputs. Every required cost must be
derivable from the addressed sources without estimation or invention.

Produce the complete vector in the natural unit of each axis, never a scalar
sum of unlike quantities. Each row records paid cost, hindsight-minimal cost,
and their non-negative difference. Every non-zero difference has exactly one
source-backed `realized` or `structural` classification. Zero rows and a zero
vector are valid.

The ledger is a generation-labelled, disposable projection. It is rebuildable
from its coordinate and cannot become authority over the sealed unit. Reckon
never changes a source, verdict, completion result, severity, assurance budget,
or next workflow edge.

## Refuse Without Mutation

If the exact Seal is not terminal, the coordinate is unresolved or
cross-bound, or any required cost is underivable, return the first applicable
typed refusal from `reckoning.md`. Produce no partial ledger, mutate no source
or earlier projection, and do not reinterpret the named door as permission.
