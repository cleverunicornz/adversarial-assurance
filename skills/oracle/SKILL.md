# Oracle

An oracle compares a promise to the actual and reports the gap. It is
the judge — not the test that manufactures the event, not the witness
that retains it. One promise may be judged by many oracles; no oracle
judges what no promise states.

Oracle resolution and promise grounding are independent. A comparator may
resolve and discriminate perfectly while the behavior it names remains
unwritten in `architecture/`; that is still a promise gap, and the comparator
cannot manufacture the missing authority. Likewise, an `oracle_ref` terminates
in real apparatus, never in another promise, review finding, future campaign,
or promise that an oracle will later exist.

An oracle is one component of a collapser application. `$collapser` binds
the comparator to the live possibility, exact instrument identity,
applicability, fidelity, witness, gate placement, and residual. A valid
oracle ref alone does not prove that the containing application reaches
or faithfully models the promised boundary.

## Where Oracles Live

- Contract-resolvable standing apparatus on main — committed comparators: tests with
  decisive assertions, checkers, validators, differential harnesses.
- Contract-resolvable genesis apparatus on a named 2000 branch at a pinned SHA — the spike
  rig, which is the first draft of the future gauge.
- Review-local apparatus under `runs/7000/<run-id>/` at immutable proof and
  validation commits. It may judge that human-invoked campaign only after
  both-polarity controls; it cannot satisfy a 3000 lock or become a delivery
  gate by implication.
- Nowhere else. An oracle that exists only in prose, memory, or a transcript
  resolves to nothing. The contract seam accepts only the first two homes
  (`assurance-runs-validator resolve-oracles` is its door).

## Origination

- Oracle genesis is 2000 work: the rig that produced a spike's verdict
  is the oracle's birth, and it rides the promotion package as the
  gauge's first draft.
- Genesis apparatus proves that a question can be classified; it does not turn
  the question or its promotion package into an architecture promise.
- Oracle modification is classified as possible 2000 work: if separately
  commissioned there, elicit the new discrimination and witness that every
  retained kill still kills.
- A 7000 campaign may originate or modify a review-local comparator under its
  independent executor/validator proof pair. Promotion of that comparator
  into delivery apparatus is possible 2000 work under a separate human
  commission; the review archive is not contract-resolvable genesis.
- A missing or changed comparator for an already grounded Promise blocks the
  present contract and is classified as possible 2000 work. A missing
  architecture Promise is classified through `$promise`. Neither finding
  creates its possible predecessor, and a settled Promise gap does not become
  a spike merely because the oracle seam noticed it.

These classifications create no branch, unit, pass, review, qualification, or
follow-on act. Only an express human commission does.

## Deriving An Oracle From A Promise

1. Take one promised behavior and name its **decisive observable** —
   the event distinguishing kept from broken: a typed rejection, an
   invariant at a gate, a byte-identical replay.
2. Choose the comparator shape that can see it: decisive assertion,
   differential (model vs runtime), checker over a recorded history,
   snapshot with stabilized fields.
3. Build it as apparatus at a lawful home — standing beside the
   surface it judges, or genesis in the campaign's quarantine.
4. Prove it with known-answer controls, both polarities.
5. Record the ref a contract can resolve: a worktree path, or
   `<git-ref>:<path>` at a pinned SHA.

Worked (schematic, same chain as `$promise`): promise — oversize writes
refused with typed `RejectedTooLarge`. Observable: the exact error type
at the seam. Comparator: a seam-level test asserting that type.
Known-answer: with the guard deliberately removed the test must FAIL;
restored, it must PASS. Ref: the committed test's path.

## Proof — Known-Answer Controls

An unproven instrument measures nothing; its output is not data. Before
any new or modified oracle judges anything, it passes controls in both
polarities: it must FAIL against a deliberately broken input and PASS
against a clean one. One polarity alone proves nothing — a judge that
convicts everyone, or acquits everyone, is not judging.

Each control qualifies only the predicate it reaches. A known-bad control
preserves every prerequisite and non-target acceptance relation and varies
the decisive relation alone. An artifact rejected for malformed grammar,
missing fields, or inconsistent hashes qualifies those checks, not a later
producer-origin claim. When the claim attributes an actual to a producer, the
known-good actual is producer-bound under `$witness` and emitted by that exact
producer.

Known-answer subjects may be synthetic or quarantined before any delivered
product exists. Their provenance does not by itself decide whether the
containing application reaches the commissioned boundary; `$collapser` owns
that subject-origin judgment. Oracle resolution, comparator qualification, and
application suitability are independent relationships.

Controls qualify the exact instrument identity, not a product name.
Version or hash, configuration, flags, exclusions, baseline, topology,
seed policy, and comparator all participate. `$collapser` owns recording
that full application identity and its fidelity boundary.

## The Gap Protocol

A reported gap has three suspects — the code, the promise, or the
instrument — interrogated in the open before anything is displaced
(root law; `$gravity` is the posture). Repairing the instrument to
silence it, or narrowing the promise to dodge it, are displacement
disguised as remediation.

## Pinning

When a delivery gap closes inside the commissioned Closure, the oracle that judged it is pinned at the
gate placement named by its collapser application: committed beside the
surface it judges, named in the contract's validation expectations, and
re-run only by a validation pass already commissioned at that gate. A
review-local oracle is pinned only as immutable campaign evidence; human
acceptance may classify standing adoption as possible 2000 and 3000 work but
creates neither. Qualification alone does not
silently create a repository-wide blocking gate; placement is a distinct
contract or human decision. Once placed, a pinned delivery oracle is never
weakened, skipped, or un-advertised as a fix. Decay reopens gaps; pinned
oracles are how re-derivation stays cheap.

Running a named comparator to manufacture terminal evidence does not
commission proof of the comparator's compiler, runner, host, network,
dependencies, or other supporting means. Those subjects enter only when a
positive-depth Closure acceptance row names them.

## Relationships

- Born from promises (`$promise`); routed by the contract's Collapse
  Route (`$collapse-graph`); bound into an executable application by
  `$collapser`; its judged events are retained by witnesses
  (`$witness`); its resolution gates the 3000 lock (3010).

## This Is NOT

- Not the witness plane: it defines no retention or evidence shapes.
- Not the collapser application: a comparator can discriminate while
  being inapplicable, configured differently, or credited past its
  fidelity domain.
- Not the collapse legend: instruments are commissioned there; this
  skill governs how any of them becomes a judge.
- Not a coverage or ceremony mandate: one decisive comparator
  outweighs a hundred assertions that observe nothing decisive.
