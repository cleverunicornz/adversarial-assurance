# Lane 8000 - Materialization

The optional follow-on that puts terminal behavior where it runs, and the
direct lane that brings a commissioned machine substrate online. Test
apparatus and Moon development deployment used to prove Build remain in their
parent lane; they do not create durable machine authority.

For new product behavior, nonterminal behavior refuses and routes to 4000.
Before any operational mutation, require the exact human disposition owned by
`architecture/fleet-substrate/AGENTS.md` without restating its grammar.

Silence, historical placement, installed-package inspection, and agent
inference refuse as `HOST_PROFILE_DISPOSITION_REQUIRED`.

Environment is binding. Development materializes through checked-in native
Ubuntu configuration and never uses Nix. Staging uses Nix to build, publish,
substitute, activate, and prove one exact closure. Production promotes that
same staging-proven closure without rebuilding it. An 8000 unit may not cross
those authorities or turn a development receipt into a Nix closure.

Flow: product materialization follows terminal 4000 behavior; machine
bootstrap and maintenance may enter directly with their operational owner.
Both pass the human capability/environment gate. A later 6000 unit reconciles
the operational fact created here.
